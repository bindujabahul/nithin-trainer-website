About Me

I am a certified personal trainer in Thrissur with over 9 years of experience helping individuals transform their health, fitness, and lifestyle. My approach is focused on creating personalized programs that are practical, result-oriented, and sustainable for long-term success. I specialize in a wide range of fitness services including Personal Training, Weight Management, Athletic Coaching, Medical Exercise Specialist programs, Diet Consultation, Injury Prevention, and Corrective Exercise. Whether your goal is fat loss, muscle gain, improving athletic performance, or recovering safely from injuries, I design training plans tailored specifically to your needs. Over the years, I have worked with clients of different fitness levels—from beginners to advanced athletes—guiding them with proper technique, structured workouts, and continuous motivation. My focus is not just on workouts, but on building discipline, confidence, and a healthier lifestyle. I offer both online coaching and onsite training, ensuring flexibility and convenience while maintaining professional guidance and support throughout your fitness journey.
